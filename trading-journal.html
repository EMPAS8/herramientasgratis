Bring in your MT5 history

The journal sorts your trades into Manual and each EA, then tracks profit and stats for each one separately.

  1. In MetaTrader 5, press Ctrl+T and open the History tab.
  2. Right-click the list and pick the period you want (for example All history).
  3. Right-click again, choose Report, then Open XML (MS Excel), and save the file.
  4. Press Add MT5 report and choose that file, or drop it anywhere on this page.

Later, press Download journal to save everything (trades, notes, your rules) in one file. Open that file next time and add newer reports on top; trades already in the journal are skipped.

Trades

Times are your broker's server time. Net is profit plus commission plus swap. Change a trade's source if the automatic sorting got it wrong, and add notes as you go.

Times and hour-of-day stats use your broker's server time. This is a record of past behavior, not financial advice.
